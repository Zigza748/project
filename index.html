Who we are

More than an organization.
A community where everyone belongs.

Ka-Barkada Cultural Alliance (KCA) is a Utah-based 501(c)(3) nonprofit organization dedicated to celebrating culture, expanding opportunities, developing future leaders, and serving communities in need.

The word “Barkada” is a Filipino term for a close circle of friends, a group connected through friendship, belonging, and shared experiences.

That spirit is at the heart of who we are.

Ka-Barkada welcomes people from all cultures, backgrounds, and walks of life. Through cultural celebrations, educational opportunities, youth leadership, humanitarian initiatives, and community partnerships, we create spaces where people can learn from one another and turn connection into action.

Our mission and vision

01

Building bridges through culture, education, leadership, and service.

Our mission is to bring diverse communities together by celebrating cultural heritage, expanding educational opportunities, developing future leaders, and mobilizing people to serve communities in need.

A world where differences create connection.

We envision communities where people from every cultural background feel seen, respected, celebrated, and empowered, and where diversity becomes a source of connection rather than division.

What guides us

Kultura. Akademya. Bayanihan.
Three principles guide our mission and everything we do.

Kultura

Celebrate culture. Build understanding.

We celebrate the traditions, stories, arts, languages, food, music, and experiences that make every culture unique.

Akademya

Education creates opportunity.

Through scholarships, mentorship, educational programs, professional development, and leadership opportunities, we help people discover their potential.

Bayanihan

Together, we can do more.

We bring the Filipino tradition of people coming together into our service projects, humanitarian initiatives, partnerships, and everyday work.

Why Ka-Barkada?

Belonging
without borders.

You don't have to share the same culture to share the same community.

You don't have to be Filipino, speak a particular language, or come from a particular country. You simply need a willingness to learn, connect, serve, and celebrate alongside others.

Culture should connect us—not separate us. Underneath our different traditions, stories, languages, and backgrounds is something we all share: our humanity.

Our impact

Community in action

Small actions become powerful when we do them together.

Every cultural celebration, scholarship, volunteer hour, partnership, donation, and act of service contributes to something bigger.

Our impact is found in the student who discovers a new opportunity, the child who sees their culture celebrated, the volunteer who discovers the joy of serving, the community that receives support during a difficult time, and the friendships formed between people who may never have met otherwise.

Partnerships

Better together.

Strong communities are built through partnership. Ka-Barkada works alongside educational institutions, nonprofits, businesses, cultural organizations, artists, community leaders, volunteers, sponsors, and donors who believe in bringing people together.
